
# Academic Project
This repository contains a web-based Point of Sales (POS) system developed as part of a university Software Engineering course. The project was created for educational purposes to demonstrate software development practices, system design, and implementation of business information systems.
# Web-Based Point of Sales (POS) System for SMEs

## Overview

This project is a web-based Point of Sales (POS) system designed to support Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in managing sales transactions, inventory, cgitustomer data, and business reporting through a centralized platform.

Unlike traditional manual systems or standalone desktop applications, the web-based architecture enables easier accessibility, centralized data management, and improved operational efficiency. The system allows administrators to manage products, users, and member information directly from a PC through a web interface.

## Key Features

### Sales Transaction Management

* Record and process sales transactions in real time.
* Automatically update inventory based on completed transactions.
* Maintain transaction history for reporting and analysis purposes.

### Inventory Management

* Manage product information and stock levels.
* Monitor inventory availability to reduce stock discrepancies.
* Support efficient stock planning and replenishment.

### Member Management

* Store and manage customer/member information.
* Track purchase history for each registered member.
* Support customer loyalty and retention initiatives.

## Business Analytics and Reporting

### Monthly Sales Summary

The system automatically aggregates transaction data on a monthly basis and generates reports containing:

* Total revenue (omzet)
* Net profit accumulation
* Product sales volume

These reports help business owners evaluate business performance, estimate operational costs, and plan inventory requirements for future periods.

### Member Profitability Analysis

The system provides customer-based transaction analysis by:

* Filtering transaction records based on member identity.
* Retrieving detailed purchase history for individual members.
* Calculating the net profit generated from each member's transactions.

This feature enables business owners to identify high-value customers, evaluate pricing strategies, and develop data-driven customer loyalty programs.

## Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to provide SMEs with an integrated sales and business intelligence solution that improves transaction management, enhances reporting accuracy, and supports data-driven decision-making through customer and profitability analytics.
